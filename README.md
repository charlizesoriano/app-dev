# Harry Potter

**Harry Potter** is my favorite series. It's a *fantasy* book series by **J.K. Rowling**, first published in 1997 and later adapted into a film series.

---

## Premise

Harry Potter is an orphan who learns on his eleventh birthday that he's a **wizard**. He starts at *Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry*, makes lifelong friends, and faces the dark wizard **Voldemort**.

> Love and friendship can be stronger than the darkest magic.

## The Main Trio

1. Harry Potter – The Boy Who Lived
2. Hermione Granger – The brightest witch of her age
3. Ron Weasley – Loyal best friend

## The Hogwarts Houses

- Gryffindor
  - Known for bravery
  - Harry's house
- Hufflepuff
  - Known for loyalty
- Ravenclaw
  - Known for wisdom
- Slytherin
  - Known for ambition

## Why I Like It

- Magical world-building
  - Spells, potions, and creatures
  - Hidden details that pay off in later books
- Memorable characters
- Themes of **friendship**, **courage**, and *choosing who you want to be*
